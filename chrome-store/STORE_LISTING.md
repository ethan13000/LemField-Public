# LemField

## Short description (manifest)

Independent Moxfield companion: deck requirements, card discovery, printing tools, and duplex-ready card sheets.

## Detailed description

Build and review your Moxfield decks with live requirements, card discovery and printing tools in one companion extension.

- Track editable deck requirements with matching-card counts and a movable sidebar.
- Explore EDHREC recommendations and Scryfall card searches. Filter by role, tags, color identity, type, mana value and price when data is available.
- Browse exact printings, group repeated artwork and add selected cards to decks you own.
- Select native deck-list entries with checkboxes to move or remove them in reviewed batches.
- Add basic lands by quantity and review a duplicate-removal plan before applying it.
- Prepare card sheets and local PDFs with uploaded artwork, cut guides and paired double-faced printing.
- Keep profiles and artwork locally. Optionally sync supported printing selections and token quantities through your Chrome account, or transfer preferences using JSON export/import.

Works on Moxfield, with an add-to-deck helper on Scryfall card pages. Deck edits require signing into Moxfield. Chrome 120 or newer is required. Sync is off until you enable it in the extension popup, and also requires browser account sync to be enabled. Uploaded artwork is not synced.

The extension uses deck/card information and your existing Moxfield session to provide these features. Relevant card and commander identifiers and searches are sent to Moxfield, EDHREC or Scryfall. See the privacy policy for details. No extension advertising or analytics server is operated by the developer.

LemField is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Moxfield, EDHREC, Scryfall or Wizards of the Coast.
