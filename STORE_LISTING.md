# LemField

## Summary

Independent Moxfield companion with live deck requirements, card discovery, printing selection and duplex-ready PDF card sheets.

## Description

Review your decks with editable requirement targets and live matching-card counts.

Explore cards using EDHREC recommendations or Scryfall queries. Filter roles, tags, triggers, keywords, color identity, types, mana and price. Preview exact editions, group printings by artwork, and add cards to decks you own.

Add legal basics by quantity and review extra copies before duplicate cleanup. Prepare local sheets/PDFs with uploaded artwork, cut guides and paired double-faced printing.

Settings stay local. Supported print preferences can sync through your Firefox account or transfer by JSON. Uploaded artwork stays on the device.

Requires Firefox 140+. Deck edits require signing into Moxfield. Independent: not affiliated with or endorsed by Moxfield, EDHREC, Scryfall or Wizards of the Coast.

## Privacy disclosure

Uses Moxfield deck information and existing Moxfield authentication for deck operations. Relevant card names, commander identifiers and card-search queries go to EDHREC/Scryfall; artwork loads from providers and Moxfield assets. Providers receive ordinary network information. No extension analytics/advertising server is operated by the developer. Include the complete privacy policy.

Optional sync consent: Firefox technical/interaction data consent is used only for optional print-settings synchronization, including its conflict-resolution device ID. No analytics or telemetry is sent to the developer. Without opt-in, no cloud sync read/write occurs; local storage and JSON export/import remain available. Consent can be revoked in Firefox add-on permissions.
